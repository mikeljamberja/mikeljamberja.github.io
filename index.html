Project Mission Statement

We believe digital infrastructure should serve human communities rather than surveillance models. Our work centers around three core pillars:

Absolute Privacy

Zero third-party trackers, zero advertising networks, and complete transparency on what little data enters our systems.

Community Led

Tools and advocacy initiatives are designed in direct consultation with community organizers and grassroots leaders.

Open Source

All codebases, frameworks, and advocacy toolkits are published under permissive licenses for public benefit.
